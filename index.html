    Process

    A golden-ratio engagement model.

    Each phase is scoped so it delivers value on its own — and sets up the next one to deliver more.

    1. 01

      Discover & Strategize

      Workflow interviews, systems audit and an ROI-ranked opportunity map. You leave with a plan even if we never build.

      ~1–2 weeks
    2. 02

      Architect

      Data flows, integration points, guardrails and success metrics, documented before a line of production code.

      ~1–2 weeks
    3. 03

      Build & Integrate

      Iterative sprints shipping working bots into your real tools, with demos, tests and human-in-the-loop review.

      ~3–8 weeks
    4. 04

      Scale & Compound

      Monitoring, tuning and the next automation on the roadmap — each one faster to deliver than the last.
